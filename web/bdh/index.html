A Dragon Hatchling (BDH) language model, trained from scratch on Tiny Shakespeare and running entirely in your browser. It reads and writes one byte at a time through a fixed-size "synaptic" memory instead of a growing KV cache.

What is BDH?

        
A post-Transformer architecture from Pathway: a network of locally
          interacting neurons whose attention is linear and lives in the synapses (Hebbian working memory). See the
          paper.

      

      

        
What runs here?

        
The official toy model from pathwaycom/bdh, trained on a laptop
          and re-implemented in plain JavaScript in its recurrent form, verified against the PyTorch forward pass.

      

      

        
What it is not

        
This is not BDH-CQ and does not show the reasoning results Pathway reports (ARC-AGI, Sudoku). It is a small
          byte-level language model: expect Shakespeare-flavoured text, not sense.
